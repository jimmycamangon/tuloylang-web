TuloyLang Local Storage Evaluation

Overview
- TuloyLang stores app data in browser localStorage under the key `tuloylang_app_data`.
- The current data model is lightweight and text-based.
- There are no images, file uploads, or other large binary assets in storage.

What grows over time
- Habit completion history: one date string per completed day
- Workout entries: one object per logged workout
- Notes and descriptions: plain text fields

What does not currently create major storage pressure
- No image attachments
- No file attachments
- No rich text editor content
- No duplicate daily snapshots
- No server cache

Estimated storage usage

Habit completions
- One daily habit completion history is roughly 4 KB to 6 KB per year
- 10 daily habits over 5 years is roughly 200 KB to 300 KB
- 20 daily habits over 5 years is roughly 400 KB to 600 KB

Workout entries
- One workout entry with short notes is often around 150 to 350 bytes
- 3 workouts per week is about 150 workouts per year
- Over 5 years that is about 750 workouts
- Estimated storage for that is around 150 KB to 300 KB, depending on note length

Overall practical estimate
- 1 year of normal personal use: very safe
- 3 years of normal personal use: still very safe
- 5 years of normal personal use: likely still safe for most users
- 10+ years: possible, but confidence drops because long-term browser retention is less reliable

Important note about browser limits
- Many browsers allow around 5 MB of localStorage per origin
- TuloyLang should stay well below that for normal single-user behavior over several years
- The bigger risk is not size alone, but long-term reliability

Main long-term risks
- Users may clear browser data
- Users may switch browsers or devices
- Private or incognito browsing may not preserve data
- Restores may become harder if future app versions change the data format without migration support
